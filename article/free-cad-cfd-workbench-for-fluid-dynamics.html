FreeCAD CFD Workbench for Fluid Dynamics

This article provides an overview of the FreeCAD Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) workbench, examining its core purpose, integrated simulation capabilities, and practical workflow. Readers will learn how this open-source tool bridges standard CAD modeling with advanced flow simulations, primarily acting as a graphical pre- and post-processor for external solvers like OpenFOAM.

The primary purpose of the FreeCAD CFD workbench is to provide an accessible, integrated, and cost-effective environment for modeling, setting up, and analyzing fluid flow and thermal physics directly within a parametric 3D CAD platform. By embedding simulation setup into FreeCAD, it eliminates the need to export geometries to disparate third-party interfaces, streamlining the design-to-simulation pipeline.

Core Functions and Capabilities

The workbench serves as a functional graphical user interface (GUI) that orchestrates several distinct stages of a CFD workflow:

  • Physics Setup: Users can define problem parameters, including steady-state or transient analysis, laminar or turbulent flows, incompressible or compressible regimes, and conjugate heat transfer.
  • Boundary Conditions: It allows engineers to apply practical flow constraints directly to CAD surfaces, such as inlet velocities, pressure outlets, wall roughness, and temperature gradients.
  • Automated Meshing: The workbench integrates with open-source mesh generators such as cfMesh, snappyHexMesh, and Gmsh, allowing users to discretize fluid domains directly from native solid models.
  • Solver Coupling: Instead of running an independent numerical solver, the FreeCAD CFD workbench natively prepares the configuration files, dictionaries, and domain partitions required by OpenFOAM, an industry-standard open-source CFD solver.
  • Post-Processing: Once the simulation completes, basic visual inspection can be conducted within FreeCAD, or the data can be seamlessly exported to ParaView for advanced vector fields, streamlines, and contour rendering.

Primary Use Cases and Significance

Commercial CFD packages, such as ANSYS Fluent or Siemens STAR-CCM+, carry substantial licensing costs that are often prohibitive for small engineering firms, academic institutions, and independent developers. The FreeCAD CFD workbench lowers this barrier to entry. It gives users the ability to perform preliminary aerodynamic evaluations, pipe flow optimizations, and thermal dissipation studies within a free and open-source ecosystem.

Ultimately, the purpose of the workbench is to democratize fluid simulation by merging FreeCAD’s parametric geometry tools with the numerical power of OpenFOAM into a unified, user-friendly interface.
