FreeCAD A2plus Workbench for Multi-Part Design

The FreeCAD A2plus workbench is an add-on module designed to combine individual components into unified mechanical assemblies. This article details the core purpose of A2plus in multi-part 3D design, explaining how its constraint-based toolset functions, why external referencing is essential for parametric modeling, and how it simplifies managing complex CAD projects within FreeCAD.

The primary purpose of the A2plus workbench is to bridge the gap between individual part modeling and full assembly creation. While core workbenches like Part Design focus on generating single, solid bodies, A2plus allows users to import multiple independent parts into a single environment and define how they fit and move relative to one another using geometric constraints.

At the core of A2plus is its constraint-solving engine. When assembling complex machines or products, parts must align precisely. A2plus provides specific constraint tools, such as:

  • Point identity and point-on-line/plane: Fixes reference points to vertices, edges, or surfaces.
  • Axis alignment: Aligns cylindrical components such as shafts, bolts, and bearings with holes.
  • Coincident planes and angles: Mates planar faces flush with each other or sets defined offsets and angles.
  • Parallel and perpendicular orientations: Locks the directional orientation of components.

A defining feature of A2plus is its file-referencing architecture. Instead of bloating a single project file by generating all components in one space, A2plus links external, individually saved FreeCAD part files (.FCStd) into a master assembly document. This modular approach preserves the single-part history and enables a true parametric workflow: any modification made to an original component file updates automatically inside the master assembly upon reloading.

Beyond component placement, A2plus helps verify form and function. By establishing mechanical constraints, designers can check for spatial interferences, verify clearances, and ensure that parts interact correctly before physical manufacturing or 3D printing.

Compared to alternative assembly workbenches in FreeCAD, such as Assembly3 or Assembly4, A2plus is intentionally streamlined. It employs a straightforward solving algorithm suited for small-to-medium assemblies, offering an accessible learning curve for engineers, makers, and product designers seeking robust multi-part modeling without unnecessary complexity.
