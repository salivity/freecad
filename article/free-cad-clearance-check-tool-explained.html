FreeCAD Clearance Check Tool Explained

The Clearance check tool in FreeCAD is a diagnostic feature designed to measure and verify the minimum spatial distance between components within a 3D assembly. This article explains the primary functions of the Clearance check tool, how it identifies spatial conflicts and tolerance issues, and why it is critical for validating digital designs before manufacturing or 3D printing.

Primary Purpose of the Clearance Check Tool

The main function of the Clearance check tool is to ensure that separate parts in an assembly maintain an acceptable physical gap relative to one another. While standard collision detection only flags instances where parts physically intersect (interference), the Clearance check tool evaluates whether components meet a specified safety margin or operational distance, even when they do not touch.

Key Capabilities and Features

  • Proximity Verification: It calculates the shortest distance between surfaces, edges, or vertices of distinct solids, ensuring that parts do not sit closer than intended.
  • Interference Identification: If two components overlap, the tool identifies the intersection, highlighting areas where geometry must be modified to prevent manufacturing defects.
  • Tolerance Assessment: It helps designers account for real-world manufacturing tolerances, thermal expansion, dynamic movement, and material flex by confirming that minimum offsets are preserved throughout the model.
  • Visual Diagnostics: The tool highlights specific regions on the 3D model that violate the defined clearance threshold, allowing users to quickly pinpoint and resolve spacing issues.

Common Use Cases

  • Moving Assemblies: Ensuring that linkages, gears, and rotating shafts have enough room to operate smoothly without rubbing against housings or adjacent mechanisms.
  • Enclosure and Packaging Design: Confirming that internal components, such as printed circuit boards (PCBs) or batteries, fit inside an enclosure with adequate margin for assembly and heat dissipation.
  • Quality Assurance Before Production: Catching design oversights early in the digital phase to prevent costly re-machining, mold adjustments, or scrap during fabrication.

By analyzing the precise physical gaps between solid bodies, the Clearance check tool in FreeCAD provides designers with the validation needed to guarantee that assembled parts fit together and operate correctly in real-world conditions.
